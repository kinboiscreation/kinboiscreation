# Reste Malgré Tout – Logo Design System

**Message:** Stay Despite Everything  
**Tagline:** Your life has more value than you think.

## Design Overview

A visual identity communicating resilience, self-worth, and grounded hope. The mark combines roots (stability, grounding) with upward reach (aspiration, growth), speaking to the act of remaining despite challenges.

## Visual System

### Primary Mark
A vertical form suggesting both grounding and emergence:
- **Roots:** Three grounded elements below, representing foundation and stability
- **Trunk:** Strong central vertical element  
- **Reach:** Upward branches reaching toward light
- **Accent:** Warm amber light points suggesting hope and value

### Color Palette
- **Primary Teal** (#1B5E4F) – Grounding, nature, stability
- **Warm Amber** (#D4A574) – Hope, warmth, personal value
- **Off-White** (#F5F3F0) – Breathing room, neutral ground
- **Charcoal** (#2C2C2C) – Text, contrast, strength

### Typography
- **Display:** Syne (bold, contemporary, French-friendly)
- **Body:** Lora (warm serif, readable, personal)

### Logo Lockups
1. **Horizontal** – Primary lockup for most applications
2. **Stacked** – For narrow spaces (mobile, sidebar, social)
3. **Mark Alone** – Icon-only use (favicon, small spaces)

## Applications
- Business cards
- Social media headers  
- Website favicon
- Print materials
- Digital interfaces

## Design Principles
- **Grounded in subject:** The mark's form speaks directly to resilience and staying rooted while reaching
- **Restrained palette:** Two colors (teal + amber) speak together without overwhelming
- **Readable at scale:** Works from favicon (16px) to poster
- **Human-centered:** Typography chosen for warmth and readability, not trends
- **No templates:** Every choice is specific to this message and audience
